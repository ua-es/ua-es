OUR FORMAT

A day off that
brings us together.

Picnics are just one format. We also meet for board games, in the park with children, on the waterfront or simply for a chat. We make friends in person, share costs and spend time with our community.

A shared contribution, not a ticket

We share actual costs for food, equipment and transport. The amount is agreed for each event.

Confirm in advance

Shopping depends on the number of people. The earlier you confirm, the easier and cheaper it is to prepare the gathering.

New faces are welcome

A friendly community. You can come for the first time and not know anyone yet.

Want a gathering in your city? Bring together interested people and we will help organise a shared picnic. Get in touch: it starts with wanting to meet.

YOU DO NOT NEED TO BRING ALL THIS

We bring the whole picnic.
Less hassle for you.

Behind a relaxing day are dozens of items and hours of preparation. We gather, load, transport and set everything up together, so you do not each have to do it yourselves.

Come for a picnic.
Without bringing
half your home.

That is what makes our format convenient: we share costs, preparation and one big table. More time for relaxing and meeting people.

Bring your own water and drinks. Personal items as needed. We will agree the rest before the gathering.

We bring all this — and much more — together

We bring a lot.
We think of even more.

  • Comfort and shade: shelters for sun and rain, ropes, clips, tables, benches, chairs and tablecloths.
  • Our outdoor kitchen: barbecue, stove, charcoal, briquettes, firelighters, grills, skewers, tongs, pots, pans, boards, knives and ladles.
  • Storage and serving: cool bags, ice packs, ice as needed, containers, foil, cling film, plates, soup bowls, cups, cutlery and napkins.
  • When evening comes: lamps, string lights, power banks, batteries, speaker and microphone, sometimes a guitar; plus flags, balloons and decorations.
  • For children: tent, tunnel, mats, drawing board, chalk, paper, pencils, balls, rackets and toys. Children remain under their adults’ supervision.
  • Things people often forget: fixings, tape, ties, scissors, gloves, cleaning supplies and rubbish bags — plus cleaning up afterwards.

This is far from the full list. Equipment depends on the venue, weather and group size. Extras, including a hookah, are agreed separately.

ANSWERS BEFORE YOU GO

Everything
explained clearly.

The main questions answered in one place. For individual circumstances, write to us with the subject and details.

Can I come alone if I do not know anyone?+

Yes. Meeting new people is why we get together. Mention that it is your first time and we will explain where to meet and who to look for.

Is this a restaurant, festival or private party?+

This is a friendly, open gathering for Ukrainians and friends. Invite your Spanish friends too: we share culture and meet different people. We share costs and relax together. It is not restaurant service: we may set the table and help cook together.

How much does it cost and what does it cover?+

We share organisational costs rather than charge an admission fee. The contribution depends on attendance, menu, venue and transport. We explain the amount and what it covers before you confirm. There is no single price for every gathering.

When should I confirm and contribute?+

The earlier, the better. We buy, marinate, vacuum-pack and prepare according to attendance. Late changes mean extra trips, shopping and costs. You may write on the last day, but availability must be confirmed.

Are drinks included?+

No. Everyone brings water and drinks for themselves and their family. We do not sell drinks at the venue. You may bring food to share if you wish; agree it with the organiser first.

Can I bring children?+

Yes. We bring a children’s tent, tunnel, mats, balls, rackets, drawing board, paper, pencils and chalk; equipment varies by trip. Children remain under their adults’ supervision. An activity leader is included only when stated in the announcement.

Do you accommodate allergies and dietary needs?+

Tell us before we buy ingredients. We will discuss ingredients and alternatives. We cannot promise an allergen-free kitchen, so important restrictions must be discussed in advance.

What happens if it rains or gets windy?+

We bring shelters, fixings and clips for sun and rain. In unsafe weather, we may reschedule or change the format. We will inform participants; check the organiser’s messages before travelling.

How do I get there without a car?+

The route depends on the venue. For Las Graveras, Cercanías to La Rinconada may be an option; we will confirm the exact route. Mention if you need a lift. Transport cannot be guaranteed.

Will there be lights, music and seating?+

We plan tables, benches, chairs, a speaker, lamps and string lights for the evening. Equipment depends on the venue and group size. Tell us in advance about accessibility or seating requirements.

What if my plans change after contributing?+

Write as soon as possible. Agree cancellation and refund terms before paying: some money may already have been spent on food and preparation. There is no universal refund rule for all events.

Can I help or offer my talent?+

Yes: with shopping, transport, shelters, the table, cooking and cleaning. We also welcome musicians, hosts, craftspeople, photographers and people with ideas. Choose the subject in the form.

Can you organise a gathering in my city?+

Yes, anywhere with enough interest and a suitable venue. Choose a destination or tell us your city and roughly how many people are interested. This does not reserve a place or confirm an event.

What happens after I fill in the form?+

The form prepares a message with your name, contact, subject and details. You review it and send it yourself to the organiser in Telegram. Preparing the message does not confirm attendance.

Why do you need my name and contact details?+

So the organiser knows who is writing, can reply and arrange your participation. Contact fields are not published or stored here. Future meetup updates are optional and require separate consent.
